A curated list of tools, models, and services for AI video generation, editing, and post-production.

## Video generation

- **[Pavo AI](https://pavoai.app)** — hosted text-to-video / image-to-video workspace with presets for shorts, ads, and product clips.
- [ReelAI](https://tryreelai.com/) — creates short AI videos from photos using templates; paid subscription.
- Runway — hosted generation suite.
- Pika — short-clip generator with style presets.
- Luma Dream Machine — hosted text/image-to-video.
- Kling — hosted generation with motion control.

## Editing and post-production

- **[Pavo AI](https://pavoai.app)** — timeline editing, captions, and auto-cut in the browser.
- DaVinci Resolve — desktop editor with AI-assisted cut and tracking.
- CapCut — consumer editor with auto-captions.
- Descript — transcript-based editing.

## Voice, dubbing and lip-sync

- **[Pavo AI](https://pavoai.app)** — multilingual dubbing and lip-sync from a single upload.
- ElevenLabs — voice synthesis.
- HeyGen — avatar/lip-sync.
- Sync.so — lip-sync API.

## Open-source models and weights

- Wan 2.x — open video generation weights.
- HunyuanVideo — open video model.
- CogVideoX — open video model.
